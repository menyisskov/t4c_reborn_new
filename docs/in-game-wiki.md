# In-game World Wiki

Open with **Ctrl+Shift+B**, or select **Wiki** on any character journal window.

The wiki opens on **Guides -> Leveling Guide**. Its overview links to four chapters:
levels **1-150**, **150-250**, **250-350** and **350-400**. Select a chapter in the
left list, or search for leveling, levelling, XP, farming or a level number.
The routes cover first-life and rebirthed characters, class priorities, access
quests, safer training alternatives and endgame options.

Guide farm statistics are rebuilt when an article is opened using the current
monster registry, XP curve, combat/server rates and hunting manifest. Full-kill
XP includes damage credit and excludes personal XP bonuses; next-level kill
estimates assume zero existing progress and full credit. Related monster,
field, quest and NPC links use the normal wiki navigation and Back history.
Reading a guide never unlocks travel or changes character progress.

Items, spells, monsters, NPCs, quests and areas are read directly from the loaded game registries, including JSON content. Search matches names and internal content keys, within the selected category. Click a result to read its article; click a related entry to follow a drop source, spell, quest or hunting field. Back returns to the previous article.

- Type in the search field; Ctrl+F focuses it and Clear search resets it.
- Scroll over the list to browse results, or over the article to read more.
- Up/Down selects results. Page Up/Down scrolls the article.
- Esc or Ctrl+Shift+B closes the wiki. Game movement and macros are suppressed while typing.

Monster drops show individual per-kill chances. Artherk and Ogrimar also explain their guaranteed alignment-item and grouped wearable rolls. Monster articles display base kill XP and the combined combat/server rate separately. Spell effect formulas are shown because effect-driven spells do not store their actual damage in the fixed min/max fields.

Only registered sources are listed; runtime script decisions are not treated as guaranteed shop stock or rewards. The wiki does not modify player progress, grant items, learn spells, or bypass travel unlocks.
