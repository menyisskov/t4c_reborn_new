# Adventure Journal

Open **Ctrl+J**, or the **Adventure** journal tab.

## Equipment collection

All registered equippable items are organized into divine relic collections, Concord build sets, Wardkeeper event sets and equipment-slot groups. Successful acquisition records a permanent discovery. Existing inventory, equipped gear and personal storage are recorded when loading. Selling or rebirthing preserves discoveries; previously sold equipment cannot be inferred from old saves. Completed collections unlock an optional cosmetic overhead title. Click missing pieces to inspect their wiki sources.

## Progression tracker

Suggestions use current level, armor, build, spell requirements, regional unlocks, story state and partially discovered collections. Pin a goal to the HUD; coordinates are included for hunting grounds and events. Suggestions never unlock travel or guarantee encounter safety.

## Repeating world events

| Event | Center, world 0 | Suggested level / AC | Final reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Broken Boneward | 3700, 1450 | 100 / 150 | 100,000 gold + 15M XP + build-specific signet |
| Silkfall at the Pilgrim Road | 3805, 1580 | 140 / 150 | 150,000 gold + 35M XP + build-specific pendant |
| The Iron Bell Rebellion | 5280, 1290 | 180 / 250 | 250,000 gold + 75M XP + build-specific mantle |

Each begins automatically when a living character enters its field with the island unlocked. Restore three labeled wardstones by approaching and pressing **E** within three tiles. Hold the ward for 90 seconds against three waves, then defeat the marked attacking champion within three minutes. Restoration and defense checkpoints each award an additional quarter of the final gold and a third of the final XP. Event XP is an exact award, separate from the creature kill XP/server multiplier.

Success reclaims the camp for ten minutes, granting +20 AC near Witness wards and +40 AC near the Threnody ward. Failure regroups after three minutes. Timers persist through relogging; expired offline defenses fail. Reinforcements are transient and do not respawn or replace authored encounters. Existing training-field bosses remain separate and keep their normal drop rules.

Choose Warrior, Archer, Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Light or Dark in the event journal and claim the earned equipment. Unclaimed choices persist, including when the backpack is full. Each style has a three-piece Wardkeeper collection. All 24 rewards follow ItemBalance requirements and boost budgets and are also listed in the wiki.

The current game is local: event state and participation belong to the saved character. Public multi-player participation/scaling is not implied. Progress is stored in namespaced quest flags for compatibility with existing character saves.
