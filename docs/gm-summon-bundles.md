# GM summon bundles

These shortcuts add items directly to the backpack and save once per command. They
require the same GM rank as ordinary `.summon item` commands and can be repeated.
They do not change alignment, quest flags, level, gold, or completed objectives.

| Command | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `.summon azure_armor_set` | Azure Plate armor, boots, gauntlets, helm, leggings, and protector (shield), one each. |
| `.summon good_seraph_quest` | Artherk's Will, Heart and Soul; the crafting materials and completed hand-ins listed below. |
| `.summon evil_seraph_quest` | Ogrimar's Skull, Blood and Bones; 3 Demonic Blood, 2 Foul Potions, 5 Black Widow Venom, 2 Essence of Bloodlust, and 4 Necromantic Scrolls. |

The good bundle includes 1 Fang of True Resolve, 3 Hammers of Finality, 2 Drums of
Fate, 2 Gems of Courage, 4 Tomes of Valor, 3 Swords of Majesty, 3 Rings of Pure
Faith, 2 Staves of Hope, and 2 Pearls of Wisdom. It also includes materials to
craft those hand-ins: 3 Fine Steel Warhammers, 3 Scrolls of Enchantment, 2 Finely
Crafted Drums, 8 Gems of the Immortal, 8 Hourglasses of Essence, 18 Raw Crystals,
15 Red Spellbooks, 9 Blades of Heroism, 4 Magic Scripting Kits, 4 Chaos Keys,
4 Blank Magical Tomes, 3 Rings of Faith, 3 Grails of Purity, 6 Flasks of Holy
Water, 6 Flasks of Crystal Water, 10 Symbols of Hope, and 4 Demon Tree Wood.

Both ingredients and finished components are deliberate: a GM can test individual
crafting steps or go straight to the final component hand-in. Normal dialogue,
story prerequisites, crafting fees and rebirth rules still apply.

Every reference is resolved before inventory is changed. A missing item rejects
the whole bundle rather than granting an incomplete set.

## Empyrean armor

Use `.summon empyrean_armor_set archer`, replacing `archer` with `warrior`,
`fire`, `water`, `air`, `earth`, `light`, or `dark` as needed. Each command grants
one armor, boots, gauntlets, helmet, leggings and protector of that variant.
Missing or unknown variants show usage without granting items or saving.
`ephemeral_armor_set` and `ephymeral_armor_set` also work as spelling aliases.
