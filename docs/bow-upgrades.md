# Bow +4 and +5 variants

Every registered weapon bow with a +3 now also has +4 and +5. Twelve missing variants were added for Ashwood Reflex, Elm Recurve, Hickory Compound, Oak Composite, Cedar Composite and Lancewood Composite bows. Existing Black Locust Composite +4/+5 definitions are preserved.

Each new variant keeps its +3 family's appearance, weight, STR/agility and other requirements, attack delay, and arrow-damage coefficient. Dice size and flat damage increase by about 15% per enchantment step. Archery bonuses follow the existing +4/+5 convention: 65% and 80% of trained archery; agility bonuses follow ItemBalance. New boost and item IDs are unique.

Quartermaster Elenna sells the twelve additions at Avalon Sanctuary (4068,1510). GM summon keys use the usual suffix, e.g. `.summon item item.elm_recurve_bow_5`.

The approved AoE quivers are installed; see docs/aoe-quivers.md.

Primordial Tension Ultimate (both registered appearances) now deals 397–550 base damage,
plus three times arrow damage, and grants 50% of trained Archery. Requirements remain
165 STR / 690 AGI, with a 750 ms attack interval.
