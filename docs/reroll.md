# Character reroll

Type `.reroll` in chat. Every character can reset their own allocation; arguments
or another character's name are rejected. `.help` lists the command.

The command keeps the current level, XP, rebirth count, earned maximum HP/mana,
gold, inventory, quest progress and cosmetic choices. Equipped items move into
the backpack, including a single copy of each mirrored two-handed weapon.

All five attributes return to the current rebirth's starting value. Available
stat points become `5 × (level − 1)`, and available skill points become
`15 × (level − 1)`. Skills and learned spells are cleared; the free Attack and
Archery bonuses granted by rebirth are restored. Quickslots and active buffs
are cleared, and combat, hiding and meditation stop.

Purchased elemental powers and resistances reset, and the current rebirth's
energy budget is restored. The player returns to the rebirth arrival point
**1315, 920, floor 1**. Speak to **Alphan**, say **assist**, and allocate energy
with his associates before distributing ordinary stat points. After spending
the energy, speak to Alphan again and say **ready** to return to Lighthaven.
Characters with no rebirth receive no energy points and can finish Alphan's flow
without spending any.

Each successful command saves once. Repeating it rebuilds the same budgets;
it does not add another set of points, rebirth rewards or items. It does not
refund gold paid for earlier lessons.
