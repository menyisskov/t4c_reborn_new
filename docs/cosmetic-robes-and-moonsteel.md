# Robes and Moonsteel equipment

Inventory → Robe wardrobe selects one of six appearance-only robes, or restores equipment appearance. The cosmetic.robe quest flag stores the choice without changing item slots, armour class, bonuses, requirements or combat formulas. It survives saves and rebirth. Both character models use complete native robe rigs with new embroidered materials.

Quartermaster Elenna in Avalon Sanctuary at **4068, 1510** sells Moonsteel cuirass, sabatons, gauntlets, helm, greaves, moonbow, halberd and greatsword. Armour also requires 350 endurance and 700 strength. The bow requires 700 agility; the greatsword requires 700 strength; the halberd requires 750 strength. The halberd and greatsword occupy both hands, while the bow allows a quiver. Bonuses follow ItemBalance. No new recovery-supply drops are introduced.

Equipment uses attribute requirements only. There are no character-level gates on equipping or buying items. Equipped items remain equipped when loading a low-level character. Spell learning also uses intelligence and wisdom rather than character level; normal prices, skill-point costs and spell prerequisites still apply. No save DTO schema change is required.

Approved source art and manifests are under docs/content-ideas/cosmetic-robes and cosmetic-equipment.
