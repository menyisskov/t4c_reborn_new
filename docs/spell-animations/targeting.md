# New spell target counts

Area attacks hit up to 8 eligible enemies. Single-target player attacks hit the primary enemy at full damage and up to 7 enemies on adjacent tiles at 25% damage. Range, line of sight and combat restrictions apply. The current client does not implement party-wide healing.
