AoE quiver progression

Approved quivers use attribute requirements only. The target layout is illustrative; the small impact uses a brief flash and six fading sparks.

Ten tiers span 15 to 1,000 agility, with no minimum character level. Quivers remain reusable ammunition in the off-hand slot.

  • Primary target takes the normal bow hit. Nearby enemies take the listed percentage of that hit's damage.
  • Target count includes the primary enemy; eight is the maximum. Select nearest eligible enemies, with line of sight and sanctuary restrictions.
  • Splash triggers on every successful bow hit. Misses cause no splash. Bursts do not chain, hit friendly NPCs, duplicate on-hit procs, or add crowd-control effects.
  • The simple impact adds no freezing or stunning.
  • Access: Fali sells tiers 1–3, Rawlin sells tiers 1–6, and Quartermaster Elenna sells all ten. Only attribute requirements apply. Strength follows bows in the same agility range.
