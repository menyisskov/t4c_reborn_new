Proposed XP curve

Level 200 → 201 · Drake’s Lair

Required XP: 19,710,821,719 using the applied curve and 1× XP. This is an offline simulation; its modeled level cost now matches the game.

Conditional farming benchmark: a heavily geared 50-rebirth archer survives by kiting, holds a tight pack, and spends 80% of the time attacking. Deaths, healing, defensive casts and Seraph aura procs are excluded. Pulling takes eight seconds per pack. The small percentile ranges describe random combat rolls only.

Highest XP per kill: Arch Drake: 2.1B XP, 206,623 HP, level 1000, anchor (2850,2780). Highest-XP dense field: Kraanian Dragonguard: 1.19B XP, 130,000 HP, level 750, field (2844,2714). Ten boss kills or seventeen guards are sufficient.

Build: STR 270 / AGI 1000 / END 550 / INT 270 / WIS 270; Worldbreaker Quiver, mixed offensive armor/accessories, 100 Armor Penetration / Powerful Blow / Stun Blow. Base Archery is tested at 1,000 and 2,000. Misses, armor, penetration, powerful hits and stuns use the game’s CombatResolver; splash applies to at most seven nearby secondary targets.

Arch Drake has two nominal spawn copies and a 30-second respawn, shorter than the modeled kill time; rotating copies avoids idle respawn waits. Seventeen Dragonguards fit within the dense field's initial 40 creatures; modeled pulls therefore do not exhaust the initial supply.
