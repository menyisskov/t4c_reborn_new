## 1. Inventory: what the spells actually do

### 1.1 Where spell behaviour lives

- **Data (all 132 player spells).** Every definition class is a single static `definition()` returning
  the 33-argument `SpellData`; none has per-class logic. 102 are hand-written, 30 are generated by
  `HighTierSpellCurve.attack(key, id, element, tier, shape)` (19 bolts, 11 areas). The `buff` field is
  `null` for all 132; behaviour is carried by `t4cEffects` (effect type + numbered parameter formulas)
  plus `duration`, `frequency`, `radius`, `targetType`, `attackType`, `element`, cost/exhaustion/success
  formulas and `cooldownSeconds`.
- **Interpreter (imperative, generic).** `SpellEffectManager` interprets effect types: 1 heal/damage
  (param 1 centre formula, param 2 ranged formula with `r` = distance), 2 attribute boost (param 2
  attribute name, param 3 amount), 6 summon (param 1 type, param 2 key), 9 linked-spell hook (param 1
  spell id, param 2 `OnTimer`/`OnHit`, param 3 chance, param 4 initial delay), 10 drain, 11 recall to
  respawn, 13 dispel (spell id, chance), 15 invisibility, 16/17 detect invisible/hidden, plus 3 (flag),
  12 (vaporize), 14 (exhaust/stun) used only by item/mob spells. `Player.applyBuff` turns resolved
  type-2 effects into stat contributions; `SpellAreaTargeting` holds the area and splash rules;
  `SpellCastRules`/`SpellHealRules` (already in the domain module) hold validation and heal rolls.
- **Imperative, per-spell (the only special cases).** In `MainGameScreen`: `renew_armor` re-lays the
  learned members of a fixed 10-spell protection list; `tame_beast` runs a timed channel
  (`TameChannel`, `TameValidator`); Emberqueen's Wrath has a special splash visual; cast routing by
  `targetType` (`castDefensiveSpell`, `castSelfCenteredAreaSpell`, `launchAttackSpell`,
  `launchPositionSpell`, `tryCastFriendlyTargetedSpell`). Projectile flight and impact timing are tied
  to the renderer (`spellRenderer.launchProjectile(..., onImpact)`).

### 1.3 Monster spell attacks

123 monster classes (of 385 with attacks; 484 definitions in total) carry 1+ spell attacks
(`MonsterDef.Attack.value3 > 0`), using 77 distinct spell ids; the top five are 10119 (40 uses), 10091
(27), 10120 (26), 10086 (25), 10096 (20). They do **not** run player formulas:

- Damage = `BaseMonster.rollMonsterSpellDamage` (hard-coded by id, from caster intelligence/level),
  then `MonsterSpellRules.balanceRawDamage` (elemental cap: level/2, divisor 4, divine judgment and
  Arch Drake budgets), then `damageAfterResistance(raw, player resistance)`; sanctuary blocks impact.
- Specials in `MainGameScreen.applySpell`: 10596 teleports the caster 6 tiles from the player; 10616
  heals the caster (`healsCaster`); four divine afflictions (18200-18203, 20 s debuffs via
  `DivineAfflictions.apply`); physical-projectile ids (`usesArmor`) go through melee damage;
  `EchoOfSelf` casts a signature player spell; `DivineAvatar` overrides `canCastMonsterSpell`.
- Selection: `pickAttack` filters by tile range (`value4..value5`), skips spells while mentally
  exhausted, then `MonsterCombatRules.chooseAttack` by percentage.

## 2. Target architecture

### 2.1 One engine, in the domain module

New pure package `com.perso.T4C.spell.engine` (listed in `multiplayer/domain/pom.xml`; no LibGDX,
`Player`, `BaseMonster` or clock access):

```
SpellEngine.cast(CastRequest, WorldView, RandomGenerator) -> CastResolution
SpellEngine.tick(EffectState, long now, WorldView, RandomGenerator) -> TickResolution
SpellEngine.monsterCast(MonsterCastRequest, WorldView, RandomGenerator) -> CastResolution
```

- `CastRequest`: caster snapshot (`CasterSnapshot`: effective and true attributes, level, element
  powers and resistances, skills, equipped item ids, mana, unlimited-resources flag, active effects,
  cooldown/exhaustion/stun expiries, known spells), the `SpellDef`, target (`Self`, `Unit(id)`,
  `Position(x,y)`), `now`.
- `WorldView` (interface the caller implements): units within a radius with their snapshots, distance,
  `lineOfSight(a,b)`, `combatAllowed(a,b)` (sanctuary), `pvpAllowed(a,b)`, unit kind
  (monster/player/companion/NPC) and `canBeAttackedByPlayer`.
- `CastResolution`: `verdict` (the existing `SpellCastRules.Verdict`), caster costs (mana, three
  exhaustion durations, cooldown) and an ordered list of **outcome events**, the only thing callers
  apply: `Damage(target, amount, element, kind{direct,residual,drain,tick})`, `Heal(target, amount)`,
  `ManaChange(target, delta)`, `ApplyEffect(target, ActiveEffect)`, `RemoveEffect(target, effectKey)`,
  `Exhaust(target, attack, mental, move)`, `Teleport(target, world, x, y)`, `Summon(type, key, x, y)`,
  `GrantItem(target, key)`, `SetFlag(target, key, value)`, `Visual(spellId, from, to, flightMillis)`.
- **Determinism:** every random draw (dice inside formulas, min/max fallback, chance rolls, success
  roll, area ordering ties) comes from the injected `RandomGenerator`, in a fixed documented order.
  Prerequisite: `DiceFormula.evaluate(Context, RandomGenerator)`; the existing `evaluate(Context)`
  delegates with `ThreadLocalRandom`, so offline behaviour is unchanged.
- One context builder, `SpellContexts.of(CasterSnapshot, TargetSnapshot, range)`, replaces the three
  near-copies (`SpellCastingService.context`, `SpellEffectManager.evaluate/casterContext/evaluateHook`,
  `OnlineCasting.context`). Today the online one omits `withSelfTrue`, `withEquippedItems` and five
  element powers; that is a latent parity bug that the shared builder removes.

### 2.2 Effect model

`SpellDef` = the 33 `SpellData` fields minus presentation-only ones resolved separately (icon,
sounds), plus `effects: List<EffectDef(type, params)>` exactly as authored. Formula strings stay text
and are evaluated by `DiceFormula` in the engine; the `r` substitution, `target.r_*` defaulting to 100
when a monster resistance is 0, and the `targetType == 5` hook context are moved verbatim.

`ActiveEffect` (persisted for characters, in memory for creatures):

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `key` | `spell.<key>` of the source spell; also the stacking identity |
| `kind` | `MODIFIER`, `PERIODIC`, `FLAG` (invisible, detect_invisible, detect_hidden), `EXHAUST`, `AFFLICTION` |
| `modifiers` | resolved `attribute -> amount` (normalized names from `normalizeBoostAttribute`) |
| `periodic` | linked spell id, chance, `nextAt`, `frequencyMillis`, `oneShot`, caster id |
| `appliedAt`, `expiresAt` | absolute UTC ms; `Long.MAX_VALUE` = unlimited |
| `casterInt`, `casterWis` | non-zero only for external casters (temple blessing), as offline |

Rules, all copied from single-player:

- **Stacking/refresh:** one effect per `key` per target; recasting replaces modifiers and expiry
  (`Player.applyBuff` refresh), never adds. Different spells with the same attribute add up.
- **Resolution time:** modifiers are rolled once at cast. Offline re-derives buff amounts from the
  character on every load (re-rolling dice such as Bless's `1d(self.wis/4)`); online stores the
  rolled amounts. Open question Q3.
- **Expiry and logged-out time:** offline saves remaining seconds, so a buff does not run down while
  the game is closed. Online stores absolute `expiresAt` and, at join, shifts every finite expiry by
  `join - lastSettledAt` (the same anchor idea as `regen_anchor`). Spell cooldowns and exhaustion are
  not shifted (QUEST_ABILITY_DESIGN 4.3 already made them absolute).
- **Periodic ticks:** the server simulation loop calls `SpellEngine.tick` per affected unit when
  `now >= nextAt`, with `nextAt += frequency` (offline: `now + frequency`, so late ticks do not
  catch up; online keeps that rule, one tick per pass). Default frequency 1000 ms, `initialDelay = 0`
  means one frequency, `duration <= 0` means a one-shot hook, chance roll `nextInt(101) <= chance`.
- **Interaction with combat:** `OnlineCombatLoadout` stops failing closed and instead folds active
  `MODIFIER` effects in, exactly as `Player` does: `armorClass` adds to AC, `skill:*` to effective skills,
  attributes before derived stats, `maxHp` raises max HP (and on removal clamps HP), `resist:*` and
  `power:*` feed spell resistances and powers, `exp` the XP multiplier, `unlimited` the free-cast flag.
  The equality test is "same `CombatProfile` as `CombatProfiles.fromPlayer` for the same buffs".
- **PvP:** spells honour `pvp` via the existing `PVP_FORBIDDEN` verdict and `safeFromPvp`. Offline never
  passes `pvpTarget=true`, so PvP spell damage is new behaviour and stays refused until the owner
  decides (Q1). Friendly-target spells on other players follow `FRIENDLY_UNIT` rules (Q2).

### 2.3 Cast validation, costs and targets

Unchanged order from `SpellCastRules.evaluate` (sanctuary, known, target kind, PvP, 20-tile range, LOS,
cooldown, mental exhaustion or stun, mana, success roll). New inputs the server must supply:
`distanceTiles` and `lineOfSightClear` from the server map. LOS must use **one** shared rule: today
offline uses `MainGameScreen.hasLineOfSight` (half-tile stepping over `CollisionManager`) and the server
`PreviewMap.hasLineOfSight`. Extract the offline stepping into `world/LineOfSightRules` (domain) and
have both call it; a property test compares them on hosted maps before the swap. Target rules: hostile
units must satisfy `canBeAttackedByPlayer` and `SanctuaryCombatRules.canFight`; area targeting uses
`SpellAreaTargeting.nearest` (8 targets, stable sort by distance, ties by unit id on both paths).
Single-target attacks keep the residual splash (adjacent, 25%, damage only, no hooks).

### 2.4 Content export (spells-v2)

`OnlineSpellContentExporter` exports **every** definition reachable online (all 132 player spells,
item spells referenced by exported items, linked spells referenced by effect 9/13, monster spell ids
from exported monsters) into `spells-v2.json`, version 2, keeping `Use` (`CAST`, `ITEM`, `LINKED`,
`MONSTER`, `GRANT_ONLY`). New fields: `radius`, `element`, `attackType`, `minDamage`, `maxDamage`,
`duration`, `frequency`, `minLevel/minInt/minWis` (learning), `visual` (projectile, impact, sounds,
`visualEffect`), and a `support` level per spell (`supported`, `stage-N`). `SpellCatalog` refuses to
cast anything whose stage is above the server's enabled stage (`unsupported_spell`), so each stage
widens an allowlist instead of adding code paths. A freshness test regenerates and diffs the file, as
for monsters.

## 3. Single-player keeps working, unchanged

Extract and share, never fork:

1. Move logic, not callers: `SpellEffectManager` methods become thin adapters that build snapshots from
   `Player`/`BaseMonster`, call the engine, and apply the events with the existing `Player`/
   `BaseMonster` methods (`applyHeal`, `applyPlayerDamage`, `applyBuff`, `applyExhaustion`,
   `setHiddenFor`, `monsterManager.spawnMonster`, `InventoryService.add`). `MainGameScreen` keeps its
   routing, renderer timing and messages.
2. Timed hooks move from `SpellEffectManager.hooks` into engine `PERIODIC` effects held by an
   `EffectBook` per unit; offline keeps one `EffectBook` per monster in memory.
3. Random: offline adapters pass `ThreadLocalRandom.current()`, so live play is unchanged.

**Equivalence proof (golden tests, before any caller switch).** A `SpellGoldenTest` in the main test
tree runs, for each of the 132 spells (and each exported item/monster spell), a fixed matrix of casters
(levels 1/50/150/350, low/high stats, with/without boosts) and targets (resistance 0/100/250, adjacent
crowd for splash and area) through (a) the **current** code path with a seeded random injected and
(b) the engine, comparing the full event list: verdict, mana, exhaustion, cooldown, every damage/heal
amount, effect amounts and expiries, summons, teleports. Step (a) needs one prerequisite seam:
`SpellEffectManager`, `SpellHealRules` callers and `DiceFormula` accept a `RandomGenerator` (default
`ThreadLocalRandom`). The golden file is recorded once from (a), committed, and then (b) must match it
byte for byte; after the switch, (a) is deleted and the file guards regressions. Extra tests: refresh
replaces not stacks; expiry boundary (`now == expiresAt` is expired); unlimited buff; residual splash
min 1; area falloff at `r = radius`; dispel chance edge `nextInt(101) <= c` is (c+1)/101.

## 4. Persistence and protocol

**CharacterProgress (JSON in `online_characters`, encoded canonically).** `CharacterProgress.VERSION`
1 -> 2, read-compatible: `Buff` gains `kind`, `modifiers` (sorted map), `appliedAt`, optional
`periodic`; new top-level `effectsAnchor` (last settle time, for the join shift); flags for
invisible/detect stay where they are (`hiddenUntil`) or become `FLAG` effects. The decoder upgrades a
v1 record by treating each old `Buff` as a modifier effect re-derived once (old rows are all empty
online today because buffs failed closed). Additive only; **no SQL migration**: schema 13 stays, since
progress is a JSON column and creatures' effects are in memory. If creature debuffs must survive a
server restart (they do not offline), that is a later additive schema 14 table.

**Cooldown keys** stay as in QUEST_ABILITY_DESIGN 4.3 (`spell.<key>`, `mental_exhaustion`,
`physical_exhaustion`, `attack_exhaustion`, `stun`).

**`POST /cast`** body becomes `REVISION EPOCH SPELL_KEY [TARGET]`, `TARGET` = `u:<unitId>` or
`p:<x>,<y>` (tile coordinates), absent = self; body cap 128 stays. Protocol bump to 6 (coordinate the
number with client unification). Reply: `{revision, verdict, manaSpent, events:[...]}` where events are
the outcome events that concern the caster's view (damage numbers on visible units, own heal, applied
effect with expiry, teleport). Refusals stay `409 <verdict>` with nothing committed.

**Commit and idempotency.** One cast = one draft from committed, regeneration- and effect-settled state,
committed once through the existing single-flight path with a fresh event UUID and the revision fence.
A cast that damages creatures uses the `commitCreatureStrike` variant generalized to N creatures (one
transaction: caster progress, creature HP changes, kills, `online_world_rewards` claims, quest credit
and loot drafts). A lost response is never replayed: the revision fence refuses a repeat, and the
rejoin snapshot shows the committed mana, effects and creature HP. Periodic ticks are server-originated
events committed by the simulation owner with their own UUIDs; a tick that loses the revision race is
retried from committed state next pass (as retaliation does).

**Snapshots.** Additive fields: per player `effects:[{key, kind, expiresAt}]` (own player gets
modifiers too, for the spell book/HUD), per unit visible `effects` (icons only), and a short-lived
`spellEvents` ring (`castId`, spell id, from, to/target, `flightMillis`, impacts) so every client in
range can draw projectiles and impacts. Clients never compute outcomes.

**Projectile timing.** Offline applies impact when the projectile sprite lands. The server resolves at
cast time and reports `flightMillis` from the same projectile speed constant, so damage numbers appear
on landing. The only observable difference is a target killed by someone else during flight; recorded
as a divergence for owner sign-off (Q4).

## 5. Monster spell attacks

`OnlineRetaliation.supported` currently excludes any monster with a spell attack, so those 123 classes
are not hosted for combat. Target: `OnlineRetaliation` picks the attack with the same filter as
`BaseMonster.pickAttack` (moved to `MonsterCombatRules.eligibleAttacks`, domain), and for spell attacks
calls `SpellEngine.monsterCast`, which wraps the extracted `MonsterSpellRules` plus
`rollMonsterSpellDamage` (moved out of `BaseMonster` into `MonsterSpellRules.rollDamage(spellId,
intelligence, level, random)`; `DivineAvatar`/`EchoOfSelf` overrides become data on the definition).
Events: elemental damage after player resistance (now including buff resistances), armour-path damage
through `CombatResolver`, caster self-heal, caster teleport (the map must validate the landing tile),
afflictions as `AFFLICTION` effects. Blockers today: `rollMonsterSpellDamage` and `pickAttack` live in
`BaseMonster` (LibGDX-bound); player resistances and buffs are not modelled online; creatures do not
have mental exhaustion state; scripted bosses (`MonsterLifecycle`, `NpcScriptRuntime`) are out of
scope until the monster engine (PARITY step 5).

## 7. Risks, open questions, dependencies

**Biggest risk: golden equivalence of randomness.** Offline draws dice from `ThreadLocalRandom` deep
inside `DiceFormula`, `SpellHealRules`, `SpellEffectManager` and `Player`; until every draw goes
through one injected generator in a fixed order, "same results as single-player" cannot be proven,
only sampled. The seam touches the most-used formula class in the game. Mitigation: stage 1 lands the
seam alone first, with offline behaviour unchanged by construction and a distribution test.

Other risks: the 5,000-line `MainGameScreen` holds cast routing and the `renew_armor` list and tame
channel special cases (merge conflicts with client unification); two LOS implementations disagree on edge
tiles; buff folding changes every online combat number at once (stage 2 needs a full melee re-probe);
periodic ticks add commit load (measure with the soak tool before stage 3 ships); per-instance item
charges versus offline per-key pooling.

Open questions for the owner:
- Q1: PvP spell damage (44 spells carry `pvp=true`; single-player never exercises it). Proposed: refuse
  until a PvP rule set exists.
- Q2: may players heal and buff other players (offline only companions)? Owner default: yes, same rules as
  `FRIENDLY_UNIT`; the engine does it, the server route waits for a two-character commit (section 8).
- Q3: buffs keep their rolled amounts instead of re-rolling on load. **Decided (owner): keep the rolled amount online; single-player is not changed** (section 8).
- Q4: impact at cast time with cosmetic flight. Proposed: accept as a server divergence.
- Q5: the 11 spells with no offline effect (curse on monsters, 3 shields, 7 gateways): reproduce as
  no-ops (proposed) or fix in single-player first.

Dependencies: **client unification** ([CLIENT_UNIFICATION_DESIGN.md](CLIENT_UNIFICATION_DESIGN.md),
its stage 4 "Spells") owns how the real spell book, quick bar, targeting cursor, buff bar and
`SpellRenderer` consume `effects` and `spellEvents`; its `PlayerCommands.cast(spellKey, TargetRef)`
(unit, self or position) maps one to one onto the `/cast` body above, and its `OnlineSpellAllowlist`
is deleted once `spells-v2` serves every stage. This design only fixes the data. The protocol number bump must be shared. **Class and combat parity** (PARITY step 4)
must open Mage/Cleric/Healer/Battle Mage creation and skills/powers (`skills["fire"]` etc.) before stage
1 is player-visible; stage 1 can ship server-side behind the class gate. **Monster engine** (step 5)
carries stage 5's scripted casters. Content changes to spells after export are guarded by the
freshness test.

## 8. Stage 2 as implemented (T4C-0270)

Resolved by `SpellEngine` (domain module) and committed by `OnlineSpellStrikes`, the same single-flight,
revision-fenced path as every other cast; `SpellCatalogV2.ENABLED_STAGE` is 2 (81 castable spells: 48 +
33). Golden: `engine-stage2-golden.txt` is recorded from single-player's own code path (cast rules, heal
roll, `resolvePlayerBuffEffects`, `resolveDurationSeconds`, Renew Armor's recast order, utility effects,
then the resulting `CombatProfiles.fromPlayer` / element power / resistance / max HP, as differences
from before the cast) for all 33 spells and four casters (plain, skilled, geared, and "buffed" - already
under Bless, Stone Skin and Mana Surge, so effective and true readings differ and a recast refreshes);
the server reproduces it byte for byte from committed progress. Mana Surge shows a single-player
behaviour worth knowing: its formula reads an effective power, so recasting it while it runs compounds
(33 -> 44); online does exactly the same.

**Persistence (no SQL migration, schema 13 stays).** `CharacterProgress.Buff` gained `modifiers`
(normalised attribute -> the amount rolled at cast time) and `appliedAt`; `CURRENT_VERSION` is 2 and the
record accepts 1 or 2. A version-1 record (four-field buffs) loads unchanged and keeps its version; a
buff without stored amounts cannot be interpreted, so it fails closed exactly as before (the only way
to have one is a record that predates this change; none exist online today). The record becomes
version 2 the first time a buff is laid. The settle anchor is the cooldown entry `effects_anchor`
(absolute UTC ms, written only while the character holds buffs) rather than a new top-level field: it
needs no change to the 18-argument record constructor used in some 40 places and behaves like
`regen_anchor`. Detection flags are cooldown entries `detect_invisible_until` / `detect_hidden_until`;
invisibility reuses `hiddenUntil`.

**Expiry and logged-out time.** `expiresAt > now` is active, `now == expiresAt` is expired. Expiry is lazy:
every reader asks for the buffs active at its own `now`; the settle in `PersistentSessions.character()` and
the checkpoint remove expired buffs and take back the maximum health they gave (`maxHp -= amount`, current
HP clamped, as `Player.removeBuffContributions`). At join every finite expiry is shifted by the time since
the last settle (`join - effects_anchor`), then the anchor moves to the join; every checkpoint of a
character holding buffs re-anchors, so a crash loses at most one checkpoint interval of "online" time (the
buff then keeps that much more time, never less). A buff with no duration (`durationSeconds` 0) never
expires and is never shifted.

**Rolled amounts are kept (Q3, owner decision).** Single-player rebuilds a saved buff's amounts from the
character on every load (re-rolling dice such as Bless's `1d(self.wis/4)`); online keeps the amounts as
cast. Single-player is not changed.

**Folding.** Effective vs true: `OnlineSpellContexts.caster(progress, items, now)` adds the running buff
amounts to effective stats and element powers; true stats, true powers and true resistances (what
`self.true_*` reads) never include them. `OnlineCombatLoadout` adds strength, endurance, dexterity,
intelligence, wisdom, armor class and the attack/archery/dodge skills; carry capacity reads buffed
strength; `OnlineSpellContexts.effectiveResistance` gives the buffed resistances for later monster spell
damage. Maximum health lives in the vitals. Anything outside `BuffAttributes.MODELLED` (percent armor
class, experience, unlimited resources, healing received, skills 9 and 29, mana) fails closed in every
consumer, so a buff is never half applied.

**Spells.** Buffs (25): `Event.ApplyBuff(recipient, key, durationSeconds, modifiers)`; one effect per spell
key, a recast replaces amounts and expiry. Curse (1): resolves like every hostile single-target cast -
rules, cost, exhaustion, nothing else - because effect 2 does nothing to a monster (Q5 reproduced as a
no-op; the golden proves the cost and the empty result). Invisibility, Detect Invisible, Detect Hidden, True
Sight (4): one `nextInt(101) <= chance` roll per effect, flags for `max(1000, duration)` ms. Dispel and Cure
Poison (2): per effect, the id is resolved with the exported `dispelTargets` map (id -> key of every spell
single-player can resolve, castable online or not), the chance is rolled exactly when single-player rolls
it (id resolves and chance > 0), and the caster's buff of that spell is removed if running. Renew Armor
(1): re-lays the learned components in single-player's fixed order, each resolved against the caster as
the previous ones left it (`SpellEngine.adjust`); a guard test compares the engine's list with the one in
`MainGameScreen`.

**Friendly targets (Q2, owner default: allowed).** The engine takes `Target.Friend(id)` for BUFF spells
whose target type the shared rules accept for a friendly unit (3, 4, 10, 12, 13, 14, 17: Barrier,
Protection, Bless, Mana Shield, Mana Surge, Major and Minor Combat Sense, Drowned Ward, Emberheart
Resolve, Earthen Strength and the ultra line); self-only buffs (target type 5), flags, dispels, heals and
attacks answer WRONG_TARGET. Formulas read the caster's level, stats and skills and the recipient's
element powers and resistances (`SpellEngine.friendContext`, the temple-blessing precedent;
single-player has no player-to-player buff, so there is no offline value to match - unit tests pin the
semantics). The server does **not** yet accept a friend target on `/cast`: laying a buff on another
player changes two characters (the caster pays, the recipient gains) and the store has no
two-character commit. Doing it as two commits would risk a free buff or a lost payment on a failure.
Open item for the next stage: `OnlineStore.commitFriendBuff` (both leases, rows locked in id order, both
revision fences, one receipt), then an `f:<player id>` token on `/cast` (additive; protocol stays 6).

**Stage-3 hand-off.** The `PERIODIC` effect work (DoT, hooks, gateways, shields) can reuse the
`ApplyBuff` expiry/refresh/shift rules for timed effects, `OnlineBuffs.apply/expire/shiftForJoin`, the
`effects_anchor`, and the strict-expiry convention. Still failing closed because no stage-2 spell uses
them (item and aura buffs do): percent armor class, experience, unlimited, healing-received and
speed/resist-percent modifiers.
